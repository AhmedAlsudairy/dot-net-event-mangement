# User guide

How to use Campus Hub, by role. (The app name is set by the Admin; this guide says Campus Hub.) The same short guides are inside the app under **Help**, in English and Arabic. Setup is in [SETUP.md](SETUP.md); every page is listed in [SITEMAP.md](SITEMAP.md).

## For everyone

- **Sign in** with your **company email address** and the password you use for your computer. Your short username also works. If the sign-in page shows a "Test mode" box, click an account to sign in as it (password `dev`); this only exists in demo mode.
- **Where you land depends on your role.**
  - People who work on the events side (Staff, HOD, Dean, Media, Research in-charge, Admin) land on **Home**.
  - People who only work in the KPI pages (the KPI roles) land directly on the **KPI dashboard** and see only the **KPIs** menu.
  - If you open a page that is not for your role you see "Your role cannot open this page" with buttons to the areas you can use. If you have no role at all you see "No access yet": ask the Admin.
- **Home** greets you and lists what is **waiting for you** for your role (requests to approve, requests returned to you, drafts, visits needing a bus, approved events to announce, new research, overdue KPI targets), then **quick actions**, your **recent requests**, **events in the next 14 days** and a **KPI snapshot**. Admin also sees the **Finish the setup** checklist.
- **My access** (user menu, top right, or "What can I open?" on Home): your roles in plain words, your department if you are a department KPI officer, the approval steps you handle, and a list of every area with a green tick when you can open it or a lock with the role it needs.
- **Language:** the button at the top switches English and Arabic (right-to-left). You are signed out after 30 minutes of inactivity.
- **Light, dark and text size:** open your name at the top right. **Appearance** has *Light / Dark / Auto* (Auto follows your phone or computer) and **Text size** (four steps). The choice is remembered in your browser. If the Admin locked the theme, these switches are hidden.
- **Phones and tablets:** every page fits small screens. Tables turn into cards, the menu folds into the button at the top left (below 1200 px wide), forms are full width, pop-ups go full screen and the Submit / Save buttons stay at the bottom of the screen while you fill a long form. The how-to boxes start folded on a phone; tap **How does this page work?** to open one.
- **Forgot your password?** Press the link under the sign-in button. If your company has a reset page, press **Reset my password**. Otherwise enter your email and press **Ask IT to reset it**; IT receives the request. The app cannot change AD passwords itself.
- **Menus depend on your role.** A menu item you cannot see is not part of your role. Roles change at your next sign-in.
- The working week is Sunday to Thursday.

## Staff

You can request an event, a student bus trip, food for an approved event, and upload research.

### Ask for a guest event
1. Press the yellow **New request** button, then **New event request**.
2. Fill in the event, company and contact details. Upload the company logo (PNG or JPG, up to 2 MB). Your name and department are filled in for you.
3. Write the event content in **English and Arabic**; it is printed on the event image.
4. Choose **One day** or **Multiple days** (up to 60). The start and end times apply to every day.
5. **Which KPI does this help?** (optional): click the field, then type a number or a word (for example `student` or `2.3.3`) to search; KPIs are grouped by domain with the strategic goal under each one. Use the arrow keys and Enter, or click. The **×** removes it; leave "Not linked to a KPI" if none fits.
6. Press **Save draft** to finish later, or **Submit for approval**.
7. Your request goes through the **approval steps** Admin set up (by default the HOD, then the Dean), one after the other. It is confirmed only after the last step approves. The event page shows where it is. You get an email when it moves to the next step and when it is decided. A rejection by any approver comes back to you with a comment.
8. After approval open the event: **Download image**, **Download QR**, **Print QR poster** or **Venue screen** (rotating QR), and press **Food request** if you need food.

You can edit a request only while it is a **Draft** or **Rejected**. A submitted request is locked until every approval step has decided.

### Request food for an approved event
Open the event, press **Food request**, enter the number of people, the meal, the delivery time, the location and notes. Admin confirms or rejects it and you get an email.

### Ask for a bus (external visit)
1. **New request > External visit (bus)**.
2. Enter the visit name, destination (and address), purpose, **which KPI it helps** (optional), **date**, **departure and return time**, pick-up point, grade or class, number of students, number of accompanying staff, and the supervisor's name and phone.
3. Press **Submit for approval**. A visit for today is allowed unless Admin set a minimum notice in Settings.
4. The approver Admin chose gets an email. When approved, the bus coordinator confirms the bus and you receive the bus details by email.
5. If it is rejected, read the comment, press **Edit & resubmit** and send it again.

The **External visits** page lists your visits and their status: Draft, Submitted, Approved (bus pending), Bus confirmed, Rejected.

### Upload research
1. **New request > Upload research** (or open **Research** and press **Upload research**).
2. Enter the title, choose **Research paper** or **Research proposal**, add the authors and a short summary, and choose the file (PDF, DOC or DOCX, up to 20 MB by default).
3. Press **Upload and send**. The file is emailed to the research in-charge and recorded. Nobody has to approve it.
4. **Research** lists your uploads. The **Email** column shows **Sent** when the mail went out. You can download your own files.

## Approvers (HOD, Dean and others)

Anyone the Admin made an approver of a step in **Admin > Approval flow**, by role (HOD and Dean are the defaults) or by name, sees **Approvals** in the menu with a number for what is waiting.

- Each card shows **Step N of M** and the step name, who already approved it, and, when a step needs several approvals, how many it has (for example 1 / 2). Click the event name for the full details.
- Press **Approve** (comment optional). The request moves to the next step and its people are emailed; the event is confirmed only after the last step approves.
- Or write a comment and press **Reject** (a comment is required): the request goes back to the requester.
- You can decide once per step, and never on your own request. You get an email when a request reaches your step and a reminder after 2 working days.
- If Admin named one HOD in Settings, only that person decides at the HOD step.
- The HOD role can also approve **external visits** when Admin has not listed specific visit approvers: open **External visits**, open the visit and press **Approve** or **Reject**.

## Visit approvers and bus coordinators

Admin can name people (by email or username) in **Settings > External visits (bus)**.

- **Approvers** see every submitted visit and a badge on the **External visits** menu. Open a visit and press **Approve**, or write a comment and press **Reject** (a comment is required).
- **Bus coordinators** see approved visits waiting for a bus. Open the visit, type the bus details (bus number, driver, phone, pick-up time) and press **Confirm**. The requester is emailed.
- If Admin turns approval off, submitted visits go straight to the bus coordinator.

## Media officer

- When an event is approved you receive an **email** with the event details and the **image attached**.
- You can also open **My events**, click an approved event and press **Download image** or **Download QR**. Home shows the approved events of the coming week.
- The image layout is fixed; it already contains the company logo, presenter, date, venue and QR code.

## Research in-charge

- Every research upload arrives by **email** with the file attached (files over 10 MB are not attached; the email links to the archive).
- Open **Research archive** (you land there if it is your only events-side role): every upload with title, authors, uploader and department, date, file name and size, and whether the email was sent.
- **Search** by title, author, uploader or file name, and press the download button to open a file.
- There is nothing to approve or reject.

## KPI people

The KPI pages (menu **KPIs**) show how the strategic KPIs are going: which department is responsible for each, its target, deadline and status. Your role decides what you can do.

| Role | What it can do in the KPI pages |
|---|---|
| **KPI manager** | Defines the KPIs: add, edit, delete, import from Excel, review items, assign KPIs to departments, manage departments, export. Does not update progress. |
| **KPI editor** | Updates target, deadline and status for **any** department; exports. Cannot change the KPI set. |
| **Department KPI officer** | Updates **only one department** (chosen by the Admin); the dashboard and tracking table show only that department. |
| **KPI viewer** | Read-only: dashboard, tracking table, Excel export. |
| **KPI team** (all-round) | Editor + manager (no delete). For small teams. |

None of these roles opens events, visits, research or reports, and a person who has only a KPI role lands on the KPI dashboard. Other roles (Staff, HOD and so on) can see the dashboard and tracking read-only unless the Admin changes it (see Admin > Access & roles).

**Tabs:** **Dashboard**, **Tracking**, and, for people who manage KPIs, **KPI list**, **Review items**, **Import / export**, **Departments**, **Permissions**. On a phone the tab bar scrolls sideways.
- **Dashboard:** overall completion, the status of every tracked target (done, in progress, not done), what is overdue, assignment coverage, department ranking, and the alignment of KPIs to the plans, domains and goals. Pick *All plans / Operational / Annual / Ejada* at the top. It follows the language and the light or dark mode.
- **Tracking:** one row per KPI with its plans. Press **Assign** (or the department count) on a plan, tick the responsible departments and enter each department's target, deadline and status, then **Save**. Filters: search, plan, status, department. On a phone each KPI is a card.
- **Export** downloads the Excel execution report; **Import / export** loads the KPI workbook (download the template first).

## Admin

### First-time setup (about 10 minutes)
1. **Admin > LDAP & Email:** press **Use standard defaults**, enter the server, service account and password, press **Test LDAP** (add a test user to try a full sign-in), then set up mail and press **Send test email**. Then **Save**.
2. **Who gets each role:** press **Fetch groups from LDAP**. **Members** shows who is in a group (with emails and groups inside it). A role button adds the group to that role; a role can have several groups. **Preview people per role** shows exactly who would get each role, and lets you add a single person by email or username. Then **Save**.
3. **Admin > Settings:** Name (and the logo card at the top), Recipients (admin mailbox, media officer, HOD email for visits), **External visits (bus)** (approvers, bus coordinators, bus email, minimum notice), **Research** (research in-charge email), **Sign-in help** (password reset page and IT helpdesk email), **Audit log** (how long to keep the log).
4. **Admin > Access & roles:** give roles to people who are not in an AD group yet, by email or username.
5. Check the **Event image** and **Email templates** tabs and adjust wording and colours.
6. **Admin > Approval flow:** decide who approves events, in which order and how many approvals each step needs. Test: create a request as Staff, approve it at every step, and check the emails and the image.
7. **Admin > Appearance:** choose the colours, light or dark, corners and fonts you want.

The home page shows a setup checklist until the basics are done.

### Admin menu at a glance
**Reports & Excel · Food request · Settings · Appearance · Approval flow · Access & roles · LDAP & Email · KPI entities · Import KPIs (Excel) · Audit log · Notifications.**

### Approval flow (who approves events)
Open **Admin > Approval flow**. Each card is one step; steps run from top to bottom.
- **Step name** is shown to everyone. **Role** lets everyone with that role decide. **Or only these people** lists emails or usernames; if you fill it in, only they can decide and the role is ignored. They still need a role in the system to sign in (for example Staff).
- **Approvals needed**: how many different people must approve before it moves on (for example 2 of 3 committee members; the number cannot be larger than the people listed).
- **Extra email**: told when a request arrives (people listed by email are always told; with none the admin mailbox is told).
- Use the arrows to change the order, **Active** to switch a step off, the bin to delete. **Add a step** at the bottom. A step with requests waiting cannot be switched off or deleted, and one step must stay active.
- Changes apply to requests that reach a step from now on. A rejection at any step sends the request back to the requester.

### Access & roles
**Admin > Access & roles** is the one page for access. It has a card for every role, in three groups: events, visits and research roles (Staff, HOD, Dean, Media, Research in-charge), the **KPI roles** (KPI manager, KPI editor, Department KPI officer, KPI viewer, KPI team) and Administrator. Each card shows who has it (the AD group plus the people you add by email), what it can do on the events side, which approval steps it handles, and a tick list of what it can do in the KPI pages with a **Customize / Edit** link. A comparison table at the bottom lines the KPI roles up.
- **Add** a person on a card by email or username; for the **Department KPI officer** also choose their department. The **×** removes them. Changes apply at the person's next sign-in.
- KPI roles have no AD group; give them here. What a KPI role may do is edited at **KPIs > Permissions**.
- **Events-only people:** to hide the KPIs menu from a role such as Staff, press **Customize** on that card and untick everything.

### Appearance: colours, theme, fonts, text size, logo, name
- **Name and logo:** at **Admin > Settings**, the **Logo** card uploads your logo (PNG, JPG or WEBP up to 3 MB; it is resized automatically; **Remove** returns to the built-in icon) and the **Name** tab sets the app name in English and Arabic. Both show in the top bar, the login page, the public check-in page and (logo) the browser tab.
- **Admin > Appearance** changes the look for everyone: pick one of eight ready-made themes (Ocean, Emerald, Royal purple, Crimson, Teal, Sunset, Rose, Graphite) or choose your own **primary**, **secondary**, **accent** and **page background** colours; choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Follow the device**; choose **Sharp / Soft / Round** corners. The preview on the right changes as you pick (with its own Light / Dark switch), warns when white text on a colour would be hard to read (the page refuses colours that are too light), and nothing goes live until you press **Save the look**. **Reset to default** brings back the original look. Tick *Everyone must use this mode* to hide the personal Light / Dark and text-size switches. The colours also apply to the login page, the public check-in page and the KPI charts; the event image template has its own colours at **Settings > Event image**.
- **Fonts and text size:** choose one font for **English pages** and one for **Arabic pages** from a built-in list (Arabic and English: Cairo, Tajawal, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, Noto Sans Arabic, Almarai, Rubik, Readex Pro, Noto Kufi Arabic, Amiri; English only: Inter, Roboto, Open Sans, Poppins, Lato, Nunito, Montserrat) or **import your own** font file (WOFF2, WOFF, TTF or OTF, up to 3 MB, up to 10 fonts; the file is checked by its content, so only import fonts you may use on a website). Built-in fonts load from Google Fonts and need internet on the user's device; imported fonts are served by the app and also work offline. If a font cannot load, the system font is used. Pick the **default text size** (Small, Normal, Large, Extra large); the preview shows English and Arabic. Deleting an imported font returns the pages that used it to the default font.

### Audit log
**Admin > Audit log** is the activity log: who did what, when and from where.
- The top shows the last 24 hours (events, sign-ins, failed sign-ins, security events, errors), a 14-day chart, and a red alert when one IP or account has 5 or more failed sign-ins in an hour.
- Filter by period (hour, 24 h, 7 d, 30 d, all, or exact dates), category, severity, action, person, success or failure, or search the details, page, IP or item (for example `Event:12`).
- Click a row for everything about it: who (and their roles), what, the exact page, IP address, browser, how long it took and a **request id**; the buttons there list everything by that person or everything from the same request.
- **CSV** and **Excel** export the current filter (up to 50,000 rows). **Live** refreshes every 15 seconds.
- It records sign-ins and failed ones, blocked access, role and permission changes, KPI changes, imports and exports, settings, appearance and font changes, approvals, server errors and pages slower than 5 seconds.
- Old entries are deleted automatically after the number of days set at **Settings > Audit log** (365 by default, 0 keeps everything). **Keeping the log** can delete older entries now; that deletion is itself logged.

### Reports and Excel
**Admin > Reports & Excel** shows live attendance, scores per event and presenter, and has Excel downloads: **Events** (every event with status, requester, KPI, approval history, attendee and evaluation counts), **Visits** (every external visit with decision and bus details), **Excel** (evaluation answers) and **KPI** (the KPI execution report). An event's own report downloads its attendance list.

### Everyday tasks
- **Food requests:** confirm or reject each one.
- **Notifications:** check every automatic email was sent.
- **Roles:** apply from the person's next sign-in. Remove a direct role with the **×** on its card; group roles are changed by changing the group or the role's group list.
- Protect the public QR page: check-in is open from 30 minutes before the start until the end; one check-in per device; optional rotating QR on the venue screen (Settings > QR and public page).

## Event attendees (no account)

1. Open the phone **camera** and point it at the QR code on the screen or poster.
2. Type your **full name** and **organization / department**, then press **Check in**.
3. Press **Evaluate this event** and rate it from 1 to 5. Your evaluation is **anonymous**; names are kept only in the attendance list.

## Questions and fixes

| Problem | Answer |
|---|---|
| I cannot sign in | Use your company email and computer password. If it still fails, your account may be disabled, you may have no role yet (ask Admin), or two accounts may share your email (use your username). |
| I forgot my password | Use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page. |
| A menu item is missing, or I see "Your role cannot open this page" | Menus depend on your role. Open **My access** to see what you can open and what role each area needs; ask Admin if you need another role. |
| The KPIs menu is missing | Your role has no KPI view access; the Admin can change it at Access & roles (Customize). |
| I cannot edit my request | Only drafts and rejected requests can be edited. |
| An approver is away | The request waits at that step and a reminder is sent after 2 working days. Admin can add people to the step or lower *Approvals needed* in **Admin > Approval flow**. |
| I do not see the Food request button | Food requests are only for approved events, for the person who made the request (and Admin). |
| An attendee sees "Check-in is not open yet" or "closed" | Check-in opens 30 minutes before the start and closes at the end (Admin can change this). |
| An attendee sees "Already checked in" | Each phone checks in once per event; go straight to the evaluation. |
| Emails are not arriving | Admin: **Admin > Notifications** shows each email and its error; fix mail settings and press **Send test email**. |
| My research upload says "Not sent" | The file was saved but the email failed; the research in-charge can still download it from the archive. Tell Admin to check Notifications. |
| The text is too small or too large | Open your name at the top right and use **Text size** (or ask Admin to change the default at Admin > Appearance). |
| A font looks wrong | Built-in fonts need internet; without it the system font is used. Admin can import a font file so it works offline. |
